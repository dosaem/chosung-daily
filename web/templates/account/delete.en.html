Guest token
The string that owns your records. It is also the backup code in "Back up my records".
Account email
The email and unique identifier of the linked Google or Apple account.
Apple refresh token
If you signed in with Apple, the connection on Apple's side ("Apps Using Apple ID") is revoked as well and the token is destroyed. If the revocation fails, the token is kept encrypted for up to 7 days for retries only, then destroyed.
Attempt history
Submitted words, whether they were correct, attempt counts, streak and solved count.
Subscription identifier
The store purchase identifier and expiry of the ad-free subscription.
Today's ranking
Your entry on today's leaderboard (both puzzle languages).

Your subscription is not cancelled automatically. To stop being charged you must cancel it separately in App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting the account leaves the store-side subscription untouched.

Server backups keep the data from before deletion for up to 14 days and then discard it (policy: What we collect, and how long we keep it).
