Hidden Vowels (오늘의 초성) runs the same server behind the web app (chosung.dosaem.com) and the iOS and Android apps. This policy covers all three.

There is no sign-up. Your records belong to a single guest token stored on your device. We never ask for your name, phone number, address, date of birth, or payment details.

1. What we collect, and how long we keep it

Guest token
A random string that identifies your records. The server issues it on your first visit or first launch and your device stores it. The backup code shown under "back up my records" is this token. Deleted when you delete your account.
Account email
If you link a Google or Apple account, we store the email address and the unique identifier the provider gives us. Nothing is collected if you never link an account. Deleted when you unlink or delete your account.
Apple refresh token
If you signed in with Apple, we keep the refresh token Apple issued so that deleting your account can actually revoke the Apple sign-in. It is discarded on deletion; if the revocation call fails we keep it encrypted for up to 7 days of retries and then discard it.
Attempt history
The words you submitted, whether they were correct, how many attempts you used, submission times, and your streak and solved counts. Used to build the leaderboard and statistics. Deleted when you delete your account.
Subscription identifier
If you buy the ad-free subscription, we store the purchase identifier (transaction ID or purchase token) and expiry time that App Store or Google Play gives us. Payment methods and card details are handled by the store and never reach our server. Deleted when you delete your account (see the Terms of Service about cancelling).
Advertising ID
Apps only. The Google AdMob SDK bundled in the app uses your device advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) for ad requests. We do not store it on our server — the SDK sends it straight to AdMob. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings, and change your choice under "Ad privacy settings" in the app where that applies.
Access and error logs
To investigate failures, request paths, timestamps, and error stacks are written to our server logs and to Sentry. Identifying values such as emails and tokens are masked. Sentry deletes them automatically according to its retention policy.

We back up the database once a day and keep each backup for 14 days. So when you delete your account the data disappears from the service immediately, but it may remain in backups taken earlier for up to 14 days before those are discarded in turn.

2. Why we use it

  • Running the puzzle, your streak, the leaderboard, and statistics
  • Linking an account so the same records follow you across devices
  • Checking your ad-free subscription status
  • Serving ads in the apps (not to subscribers)
  • Responding to incidents and improving the service

We do not use the data for anything beyond the purposes above, and we never sell it to third parties for advertising or marketing.

3. Sharing with third parties and processors

These processors handle data only within the function listed next to them.

Google (sign-in, Firebase Analytics, Google Play)
Identifier and email when you link a Google account; aggregate app usage events (puzzle completed, share, paywall view, subscription start); Google Play subscription verification.
Apple (sign-in, App Store)
Identifier and email when you link an Apple account; App Store subscription verification.
Google AdMob
Serving and measuring ads in the apps: device advertising ID and ad request data.
Sentry
Error tracking: request context and stack traces at the moment of an error.

Each company processes the data under its own published policy. When the service no longer needs the data, we also ask these processors to delete it.

4. International transfer

The processors above (Google, Apple, AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Sentry) run servers outside the Republic of Korea, mostly in the United States, so using those features transfers data abroad. The items, purposes, and recipients are the same as in section 3, and the transfer lasts as long as you use the feature.

How you can refuse depends on the feature. If you never link a Google or Apple account, no identifier or email is transferred to the sign-in provider; if you never subscribe, no purchase identifier is transferred to the stores. But the apps still send ads, usage events, and error reports even without a linked account — playing as a guest is not a way to refuse international transfer.

  • Ads (AdMob): you can refuse or change personalized-ads consent through the iOS tracking permission request and the app's "Ad privacy settings" (shown where it applies). Refusing turns off personalized ads, but the ad request itself still goes to AdMob. To stop ad transfers entirely, use the ad-free subscription or delete the app.
  • Usage events (Firebase Analytics): apps only, and there is no per-feature opt-out today. To refuse, do not use the apps.
  • Error reports (Sentry): both web and apps. We need them to fix outages, so there is no per-feature opt-out.

If you cannot accept these transfers, please stop using the service and delete your account and records as described in section 6.

5. Children under 14

The service is not for children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn that records belong to a child under 14 we delete them without delay. Please tell us at the contact address below.

6. Deletion and your rights

You can delete your account and all records at any time. This cannot be undone.

  • Web: chosung.dosaem.com/account/delete
  • App: "Delete account" at the bottom of the My Records screen

Deleting removes your guest token, attempt history, linked account, subscription identifier, and leaderboard entries. It does not cancel your subscription — you must cancel in App Store or Google Play to stop being charged. To access, correct, or restrict processing of your data, write to the contact address.

7. How we protect it

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and sensitive values such as the Apple refresh token are stored encrypted. The operator pages are limited to the designated operator accounts. Logs mask tokens and email addresses.

8. Changes to this policy

When the content changes we update the effective date on this page, and we announce significant changes in the app and on the web.
