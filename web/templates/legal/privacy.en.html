1. General

Hidden Vowels (오늘의 초성) runs the same server behind the web app (chosung.dosaem.com) and the iOS and Android apps. This policy covers all three and is published under Article 30 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

There is no sign-up. Your records belong to a single guest token stored on your device. We never ask for your name, phone number, address, date of birth, or payment details.

We do not create or process pseudonymised data (PIPA Art. 28-2), and we make no automated decisions or profiling that affect you based on personal data alone (Art. 37-2) — neither applies to this service.

2. Why we process your data

  • Running the puzzle, your streak, the leaderboard, and statistics
  • Linking an account so the same records follow you across devices
  • Checking your ad-free subscription status
  • Serving ads in the apps (not to subscribers)
  • Responding to incidents and improving the service

We do not use the data for anything beyond the purposes above, and we never sell it to third parties for advertising or marketing. If the purposes change we update this policy and its effective date.

3. What we collect, and how long we keep it

Guest token
A random string that identifies your records. The server issues it on your first visit or first launch and your device stores it. The backup code shown under "back up my records" is this token. Retention: until you delete your account.
Account email
If you link a Google or Apple account, we store the email address and the unique identifier the provider gives us. Nothing is collected if you never link an account. Retention: until you unlink or delete your account.
Apple refresh token
If you signed in with Apple, we keep the refresh token Apple issued, encrypted, so that deleting your account can actually revoke the Apple sign-in. Retention: discarded on deletion; if the revocation call fails we keep it for up to 7 days of retries and then discard it.
Attempt history
The words you submitted, whether they were correct, how many attempts you used, submission times, and your streak and solved counts. Used to build the leaderboard and statistics. Retention: until you delete your account.
Today's ranking
Your entry on today's leaderboard (both puzzle languages). The displayed name is an anonymous label derived from your guest number; your email is never used. Retention: the entry for that day is removed when you delete your account. Earlier days keep only the number, and once the guest record is gone that number cannot be traced to anyone.
Subscription identifier
If you buy the ad-free subscription, we store the purchase identifier (transaction ID or purchase token) and expiry time that App Store or Google Play gives us. Payment methods and card details are handled by the store and never reach our server. Retention: until you delete your account (see the Terms of Service about cancelling).
Advertising ID
Apps only. The Google AdMob SDK bundled in the app uses your device advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) for ad requests. We do not store it on our server — the SDK sends it straight to AdMob. How to refuse or reset it is in section 10 below.
Access and error logs
To investigate failures, request paths, timestamps, and error stacks are written to our server logs and to Sentry. Identifying values such as emails and tokens are masked. Retention: Sentry deletes them according to its retention policy, and server access logs are kept for at least a year as required by the PIPA Enforcement Decree Art. 30.

We back up the database once a day and keep each backup for 14 days. So when you delete your account the data disappears from the service immediately, but it may remain in backups taken earlier for up to 14 days before those are discarded in turn.

4. Sharing with third parties

The companies below are third parties that also process the data under their own policies and for their own decisions. We integrate only within the scope their terms and policies allow.

Google (sign-in · Google Play subscription verification · AdMob ads)
Recipient: Google LLC. Shared: Google account identifier and email (if you link one), subscription purchase token, ad request data and the device advertising ID. Purpose: sign-in, subscription validity, serving and measuring ads. Retention: per Google's own policy; what we store is deleted when you delete your account.
Apple (sign-in · App Store subscription verification)
Recipient: Apple Inc. Shared: Apple account identifier and email (if you link one), subscription transaction ID. Purpose: sign-in, subscription validity, revoking the connection when you delete your account. Retention: per Apple's own policy; what we store is deleted when you delete your account.

We do not otherwise provide, sell, or share personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing. We disclose data where the law requires it, and only to that extent.

5. Processors we delegate work to

These processors handle data only for our purposes and only within the work listed.

Google (Firebase Analytics)
Delegated work: aggregating app usage events (puzzle completed, share, paywall view, subscription start).
Sentry
Delegated work: error tracking. Collecting and storing request context and stack traces.

The operator runs the server and database directly, so no hosting is delegated. If the work or the processors change we update this section and the effective date. When the service no longer needs the data, we also ask these processors to delete it.

6. International transfer

The companies above run servers outside the Republic of Korea, so using those features transfers data abroad. Instead of asking for separate consent we rely on PIPA Art. 28-8(1)3 — a transfer necessary to perform our contract with you — and publish the disclosures required by Art. 28-8(2) below.

Google (sign-in · Google Play subscription verification)
  • Items transferred: Google account identifier and email, subscription purchase token
  • Country, time, method: the United States and other countries where Google runs servers / when you use the feature / HTTPS network transfer
  • Recipient and contact: Google LLC (published channel: policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Purpose and retention: sign-in and subscription validity / per Google's retention policy
  • How to refuse, and the effect: if you never link a Google account, or unlink it, there is no transfer. You can still play as a guest; only continuing the same records across devices stops working.
Apple (sign-in · App Store subscription verification)
  • Items transferred: Apple account identifier and email, subscription transaction ID, Apple refresh token
  • Country, time, method: the United States and other countries where Apple runs servers / at sign-in, subscription verification, and revocation / HTTPS network transfer
  • Recipient and contact: Apple Inc. (published channel: apple.com/legal/privacy)
  • Purpose and retention: sign-in, subscription validity, revoking the connection / per Apple's retention policy
  • How to refuse, and the effect: if you never link an Apple account, or unlink it, there is no transfer. The effect is the same as for Google above.
Google AdMob (ads in the apps)
  • Items transferred: device advertising ID (IDFA/ADID) and ad request data (coarse location, device type, and the other values the SDK collects)
  • Country, time, method: the United States and other countries where Google runs servers / whenever the app requests an ad / sent by the SDK over HTTPS without passing through our server
  • Recipient and contact: Google LLC (published channel: policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
  • Purpose and retention: serving and measuring ads, click-fraud prevention / per Google's retention policy
  • How to refuse, and the effect: you can refuse personalized-ads consent through the iOS tracking permission request and the app's "Ad privacy settings" (shown where it applies). Refusing turns off personalized ads, but the ad request itself still goes to AdMob. To stop ad transfers entirely, use the ad-free subscription or delete the app.
Google Firebase Analytics (app usage events)
  • Items transferred: app instance identifier, event names and parameters (puzzle completed, share, paywall view, subscription start)
  • Country, time, method: the United States and other countries where Google runs servers / when the event happens in the app / sent by the SDK over HTTPS
  • Recipient and contact: Google LLC (published channel: firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
  • Purpose and retention: counting feature usage to improve the service / per Google's retention policy
  • How to refuse, and the effect: this happens in the apps only, and there is no per-feature opt-out today. To refuse the transfer you have to stop using the apps (the web app sends none of it).
Sentry (error reports)
  • Items transferred: request path, timestamp, and stack trace at the moment of an error (emails and tokens masked)
  • Country, time, method: the United States and other countries where Sentry runs servers / when an error happens / HTTPS network transfer
  • Recipient and contact: Sentry (published channel: sentry.io/privacy)
  • Purpose and retention: finding and fixing outages / deleted automatically under Sentry's retention policy
  • How to refuse, and the effect: this happens on both web and apps and we need it to fix outages, so there is no per-feature opt-out. To refuse the transfer you have to stop using the service.

The apps still send ads, usage events, and error reports even without a linked account — using the service without an account is not a way to refuse international transfer. If you cannot accept these transfers, please stop using the service and delete your account and records as described in section 8.

7. How we destroy data

We destroy personal data without delay once its retention period ends or its purpose is fulfilled.

  • Procedure: when a deletion request arrives (web /account/delete, "Delete account" in the app, or an email request) a single transaction deletes your attempt history, streak, linked account, subscription identifier, hint records, and guest token, and removes today's leaderboard entry. There is no "kept after deletion" period.
  • Method: database rows are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered from the service; the encrypted Apple refresh token is destroyed as soon as revocation finishes, or after at most 7 days if revocation keeps failing. Backups are deleted as whole dump files after 14 days. We hold no printed personal data.
  • Access and error logs: Sentry deletes them under its own retention policy, and server access logs are deleted once the period required by law has passed.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or suspend the processing of your personal data at any time (PIPA Art. 35-37). The legal representative of a child under 14 may exercise the same rights on the child's behalf.

  • Deletion: web chosung.dosaem.com/account/delete, or "Delete account" at the bottom of the My Records screen in the app. This cannot be undone.
  • Access, correction, suspension: write to the email in section 12 below and we will tell you the outcome, or how long it will take, within 10 days of receiving the access request.
  • Representative: with a letter of authority, an agent may act for you.

Deleting removes your guest token, attempt history, linked account, subscription identifier, and today's leaderboard entry. It does not cancel your subscription — you must cancel in App Store or Google Play to stop being charged. Where the law requires us to keep records, we may limit a request to that extent and will tell you why.

9. How we protect it

We take the measures below under PIPA Art. 29 and Enforcement Decree Art. 30.

  • Encryption in transit: every web and app request travels over HTTPS only.
  • Encryption at rest: sensitive values such as the Apple refresh token are stored encrypted.
  • Access control: operator pages are limited to an allow-list of operator accounts, and state-changing requests are protected with a CSRF token.
  • Access logging and masking: server logs mask tokens and email addresses.
  • Backups: taken daily and kept for 14 days, with restore rehearsals.

10. Automatic collection — cookies, advertising IDs, analytics SDKs

Cookies
On the web we use a signed session cookie to keep you signed in and to hold the deletion confirmation form together. We use no advertising or analytics cookies. You can refuse or clear cookies in your browser settings; if you refuse them, linking an account and the web deletion page stop working. The guest token is not a cookie — it lives in your browser's localStorage and clearing site data removes it (that device then cannot continue its records).
Advertising ID (IDFA / Advertising ID)
The AdMob SDK in the apps uses your device advertising identifier for ad requests. On iOS, turn off an app's tracking request under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android you can reset the advertising ID or delete it under Settings > Privacy > Ads. You can also revisit personalized-ads consent under "Ad privacy settings" in the app where that applies. To remove ads altogether, use the ad-free subscription or delete the app.
Analytics SDK (Firebase Analytics)
Apps only; it counts screen and feature usage events. There is no per-feature opt-out — deleting the app stops the transfer. The web app has no analytics SDK.

11. Children under 14

The service is not for children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data from them (PIPA Art. 22-2). If we learn that records belong to a child under 14 we delete them without delay. Tell us at the contact address and we will delete those records without asking for proof of legal representation.

13. How to seek redress
