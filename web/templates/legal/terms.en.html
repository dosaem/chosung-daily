1. What the service does

One masked word per day. The puzzle changes at midnight Korea Standard Time (KST), with one puzzle per puzzle language (Korean and English). There is no sign-up: the guest token stored on your device owns your records. These terms are available at any time from the service and on this page.

2. Linked accounts and backup codes

Linking a Google or Apple account lets you continue the same records on another device. Without an account you can still move your records with the backup code shown under "back up my records". That code is the key to your records, so do not share it. If you lose the code and never linked an account, there is no way to recover the records.

4. Withdrawal and refunds

Billing and refunds for the subscription are handled by App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). The operator cannot see your payment method or transaction history and cannot process a refund for you — please ask the store directly.

  • App Store: report the problem from your purchase history at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play: use the refund request form at support.google.com/googleplay.

Under Article 17 of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce you may withdraw your purchase within 7 days of payment. However, Art. 17(2)5 allows that right to be restricted for the part of digital content already supplied — the ad-free benefit applies from the moment of purchase. You can still withdraw for a part not yet supplied (a future billing period, for example), and an accepted withdrawal is refunded to your payment method under the store's policy.

If the subscription did not do what it promised (you paid and ads kept showing, for example), write to the email in section 11. We will find and fix the cause and help you document the transaction for the store's refund request.
