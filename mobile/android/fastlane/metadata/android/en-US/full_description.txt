One word. Only its consonants. Five tries.

Hidden Vowels is a daily word game with exactly one puzzle a day. It takes about
thirty seconds, and a new word opens at midnight.

■ One puzzle a day
A new puzzle opens at midnight (Korea time). Everyone plays the same word.

■ Two boards
The English board blanks out the vowels and leaves the consonant skeleton
(P _ R P L _ for a five-letter word). The Korean board shows only the leading
consonant of each syllable. Switch the puzzle language any time in settings.

■ Five tries
After each guess, letters in the right place turn green and the rest stay grey.
A hint unlocks after the third wrong guess.

■ Today's ranking
See how everyone who solved today's word did, by tries and time. Names are
anonymous numbers — no email, no real names.

■ Streaks
Play every day and your streak grows. The last seven days are on the records
tab, and results share as an emoji grid.
