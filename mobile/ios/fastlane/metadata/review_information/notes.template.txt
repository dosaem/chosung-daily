APP PURPOSE:
Hidden Vowels (Korean title: 오늘의 초성) is a daily word game. Exactly one puzzle
opens per day at midnight Korea time, and every player gets the same word. The
board shows only the consonants — the leading consonant of each syllable on the
Korean board, the consonant skeleton with the vowels blanked out on the English
board — plus the word length. Players have five tries; after each guess, letters
in the correct position are marked green and the rest grey. A hint unlocks after
the third wrong guess.

HOW TO REVIEW:
- No account registration or login is required. On first launch the app asks the
  server for an anonymous guest token and that token owns the history.
- Launch the app, finish the four onboarding pages (the first page also picks the
  puzzle language), then type a guess on the "Today" tab and press Submit.
- The other two tabs are "Ranking" (today's leaderboard by tries and time; display
  names are anonymous numbers derived from the guest id, never emails) and
  "Records" (streaks, the last seven days, settings).
- Signing in with Google or Apple is optional and only links the existing history
  to an account so it survives a device change. A backup code does the same
  without any account.

SUBSCRIPTION TEST (auto-renewable, removes ads):
- The only in-app purchase is a monthly auto-renewable subscription that removes
  ads. Everything else in the app is free and no feature is behind the paywall.
- Open the "Records" tab and tap the subscription card to reach the paywall. The
  price shown is the localized App Store price (KRW 1,500 in Korea).
- Please use the Sandbox tester account we entered in App Store Connect >
  App Information > App Review Information:
      Sandbox tester Apple ID: <SANDBOX_TESTER_EMAIL>
      Password:                <SANDBOX_TESTER_PASSWORD>
  (We do not keep these credentials in the source repository. If the fields are
  empty, please contact us at the review contact address and we will provide a
  fresh Sandbox tester.)
- After a successful Sandbox purchase the ads disappear within one app launch.
- Cancellation path, as stated in the terms: Settings > Apple Account >
  Subscriptions. The app never asks for payment details itself.

ACCOUNT DELETION PATH (App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v)):
- In the app: "Records" tab > "Delete account" (red, at the bottom of the
  settings list) > confirm. This deletes the guest history, the linked account
  and today's leaderboard entry, and revokes the Apple sign-in connection.
- On the web, without the app: https://chosung.dosaem.com/account/delete
- Deletion is immediate in the service. Daily database backups keep a copy for
  up to 14 days, which the privacy policy states.

EXTERNAL SERVICES:
- Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, rewarded "preview a hint" ads).
- Firebase Analytics (four aggregate events: puzzle completed, share, paywall
  view, subscription start) and Sentry (crash and error reports).
- Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple for the optional account link.
- App Store Server API and Google Play Developer API for subscription receipt
  verification, called from our server.
- Our own backend (https://chosung.dosaem.com/api/v1 — a normal public server,
  no allow-list or VPN needed) for puzzles, guesses, rankings and history. No
  other third-party API, payment processor or AI service.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES:
Content and features are identical in every region. The app is localized in
Korean and English, and the puzzle language is a separate in-app setting, so an
English-speaking player can play the Korean board and the other way round. The
day boundary is midnight Korea time everywhere.

REGULATED INDUSTRY:
Not applicable. This is a casual word game with no gambling, no health data and
no financial services.
